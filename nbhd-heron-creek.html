The golf and the clubhouse

A club scaled for a town.

Membership, HOA, and the no-CDD advantage

Know what is required and what is optional.

The club describes itself as privately owned and semi-private, says membership is open to everyone with no residency required, and states that no Community Development District fee is attached to homes in Heron Creek. Homes still carry homeowner association dues and property taxes, and some neighborhoods inside the gate may have their own fees.

Heron Creek's homes are villas and single-family homes, many on lakes or the golf course. FAQs

Heron Creek: common questions

Where is Heron Creek in North Port?

Heron Creek is a gated golf community in North Port, Sarasota County. The club's address is 5301 Heron Creek Blvd, North Port, FL 34287, near Interstate 75.

Do you have to join the Heron Creek golf club?

The club describes itself as a privately owned, semi-private club and says membership is open to everyone, with no residency required. Ask whether any membership or fee is tied to a specific home before you buy.

Does Heron Creek have a CDD fee?

The club states there is no Community Development District (CDD) fee attached to homes in Heron Creek. Homes still pay property taxes and homeowner association dues, so ask for both.

How many holes does Heron Creek have?

Heron Creek has a 27-hole championship course designed by Arthur Hills, laid out as three nine-hole courses that can be played in different 18-hole combinations.

What amenities does Heron Creek have?

The club lists a 21,000-square-foot, two-level clubhouse with a dining room and a grille, a pro shop, Har-Tru tennis courts, a resort-style pool, a fitness center, and an active social calendar.

What schools are near Heron Creek?

North Port schools include Heron Creek Middle School and Toledo Blade Elementary, both graded A for 2025-26, and North Port High School, graded B. Confirm assignments for each address with Sarasota County Schools.
