Explore · Englewood · Tangerine Woods

Tangerine Woods is a gated, resident-owned community of 366 homes on 114 acres in Englewood, within three miles of the Gulf beaches.

It has been run by its owners since the 1980s, and much of daily life centers on a large clubhouse and a long list of volunteer-run activities. Here is what the community and the county record show. For the wider area, see Explore Englewood.

History

How Tangerine Woods began

Owner-run since the start.

The land for Tangerine Woods was bought on January 14, 1983, and the first home sold the next month. The clubhouse was dedicated on April 12, 1984. Owners began organizing in November 1983, and today the Tangerine Woods Owners Association has a seven-member elected board, operates as a condominium under Chapter 718 of Florida law, and hires a community association manager for day-to-day operations.

Source: Tangerine Woods, brief history.

Community

Life in Tangerine Woods

Coffee hour, courts, and volunteers.

The clubhouse complex includes a heated pool with a hot tub, an exercise room, tennis and pickleball courts, shuffleboard, a bocce court, and a horseshoe pit. A recreation committee runs weekly activities, a Saturday coffee hour, dinners, and dances. A volunteer group handles maintenance projects, which the community says helps keep monthly fees down. Many homes sit on waterfront lots.

Source: Tangerine Woods Owners Association; Tangerine Woods, brief history.

FAQs

Tangerine Woods: common questions

Where is Tangerine Woods?

Tangerine Woods is on the Sarasota County side of Englewood, ZIP 34223. The community says it is about 20 miles south of Sarasota and within three miles of the Gulf beaches.

Is Tangerine Woods resident owned?

Yes. The community describes itself as deed restricted, resident owned, and gated. Its owners association has a seven-member elected board and operates as a condominium under Chapter 718 of Florida law.

How many homes are in Tangerine Woods?

The community has 366 units on 114 acres, with each lot at least 60 by 100 feet. The Sarasota County roll lists 366 manufactured home parcels.

What amenities does Tangerine Woods have?

The 8,300-square-foot clubhouse, dedicated in 1984, sits with a heated pool and hot tub, exercise room, tennis and pickleball courts, shuffleboard, bocce, and a horseshoe pit. There is also a separate storage area for RVs, boats, and trailers.

What do homes in Tangerine Woods cost?

The county's median just value for a home here is about $144,000. The 43 qualified sales recorded in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $179,000.
