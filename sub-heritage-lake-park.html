Heritage Lake Park, Punta Gorda: Gated Villas and Condos Built Since 2005

Explore · Punta Gorda · Heritage Lake Park

Heritage Lake Park is a gated community of about 460 condos and villas, all built since 2005, where low-maintenance living comes with a clubhouse and a heated pool.

It suits buyers who want newer construction at a lower price than a detached home. Here is what the county record shows and what to check before you buy. For the wider city, see Explore Punta Gorda.

Community

Living in Heritage Lake Park

Shared amenities and a CDD.

One of the condo associations describes Heritage Lake Park as a gated development with a clubhouse, a fitness center, a heated pool, and walking trails shared by residents, with lanais that look out on lakes or nature. Several separate condominium associations sit inside the community, and a community development district also serves it.

Source: Lakeview II Condominiums at Heritage Lake Park Association; Heritage Lake Park CDD.

Homes

Heritage Lake Park homes for sale

Condos and villas at entry-level prices.

The county roll lists 483 parcels: 306 condo units, 154 attached villas, and 2 detached homes. Villas on the main plat sold at a median of about $250,000 across 8 qualified sales in 2025 and 2026.

FAQs

Heritage Lake Park: common questions

Where is Heritage Lake Park?

Heritage Lake Park is in ZIP 33983 with a Punta Gorda address, in unincorporated Charlotte County. Streets include Heritage Lake Boulevard, Royal Tern Circle, Heron Lake Drive, and Willow Hammock Circle.

Is Heritage Lake Park gated?

Yes. One of the condominium associations describes Heritage Lake Park as a gated development with a shared clubhouse, fitness center, heated pool, and walking trails.

What kinds of homes are in Heritage Lake Park?

Mostly condos and attached villas. The county roll lists 306 condo units and 154 attached villas, with only 2 detached homes.

What do homes in Heritage Lake Park cost?

The 88 qualified sales of homes in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $249,000. The median county just value of a condo unit is about $110,000.

Is there a CDD in Heritage Lake Park?

Yes. A community development district, the Heritage Lake Park CDD, serves the community. Ask how its assessment appears on the tax bill for the unit you are considering.
