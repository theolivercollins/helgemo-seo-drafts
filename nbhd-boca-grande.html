Explore · Boca Grande, Gasparilla Island

Boca Grande is a small village on Gasparilla Island, reached by one tolled causeway, where a home purchase comes with island rules most mainland buyers never think about.

The village sits on the south part of Gasparilla Island, a barrier island that Lee and Charlotte counties share. It is known for its historic downtown, its beaches, and the tarpon fishing in Boca Grande Pass. Buyers who start their search in Punta Gorda or Rotonda West often add Boca Grande to the list, then find that the questions are different here. This guide covers the ones our customers ask first.

Location

Where Boca Grande is, and why the county line matters

Lee County village, Charlotte County north end.

Boca Grande is a residential village on Gasparilla Island in Southwest Florida. The island is part of both Charlotte and Lee counties, and the village itself is entirely in the Lee County portion. The name, Spanish for big mouth, comes from Boca Grande Pass at the island's south tip.

The county line is not trivia. It sets which property appraiser values the home, which tax bill you pay, and which office handles permits. Homes in the village are researched through the Lee County Property Appraiser. Homes on the Charlotte County end of the island use the Charlotte County Property Appraiser. Always confirm the county on the parcel record before you compare taxes between two listings.

Source: Wikipedia, Boca Grande, Florida.

Getting there

The causeway, the toll, and the swing bridge

Your daily commute starts at a toll booth in Placida.

Cars reach Gasparilla Island on the Boca Grande Causeway from Placida. The Gasparilla Island Bridge Authority owns it, maintains three bridges including a swing bridge, and says all of its operating revenue comes from tolls, not tax dollars. Passenger vehicles pay $6.00 for a round trip at the full fare.

For an owner, the toll is a real line item. Cleaners, pool service, landscapers, and contractors all cross it, and many build it into their pricing. Homes

Boca Grande homes for sale: what the market looks like

A small, high-value, seasonal market.

Boca Grande's housing is small in number and high in value. In ZIP 33921, the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 survey counts about 1,635 housing units, puts the median owner-reported home value in its top bracket of $2 million or more, and finds about 66 percent of units held for seasonal or occasional use.

That seasonal share shapes how homes are shown, maintained, and insured. Expect showings to need more notice when owners are away, and compare recent sales on the same street or in the same building rather than island-wide averages.

Daily life

What living on the island is like

Beaches, a lighthouse, and the tarpon.

Gasparilla Island State Park covers the island's south end, with five parking lots, Gulf beaches, and the restored Port Boca Grande Lighthouse, built in 1890 and now a museum. The park charges $3 per vehicle and opens from 8 a.m. to sunset every day of the year.

Boca Grande Pass is famous for tarpon fishing, and the island's history as a phosphate shipping point and a fishing destination still shows in its old buildings. Swimming, snorkeling, and shelling are popular, and the state park notes that shelling is best in winter along the Gulf.

For groceries, health care, and bigger shopping trips, most owners drive back across the causeway to Placida, Rotonda, Englewood, or Port Charlotte. FAQs

Boca Grande: common questions

Is Boca Grande in Lee County or Charlotte County?

Both counties share Gasparilla Island. The village of Boca Grande sits entirely in the Lee County part of the island, while the north end of the island is in Charlotte County. The county decides which property appraiser, tax bill, and permitting office apply to a home.

How do you get to Boca Grande?

By car, you reach Gasparilla Island on the Boca Grande Causeway from Placida. The Gasparilla Island Bridge Authority owns and tolls the causeway and says it receives no tax dollars. The causeway includes a swing bridge over the Intracoastal Waterway.

How much is the Boca Grande toll?

The Gasparilla Island Bridge Authority lists $6.00 for a car or pickup at the full fare, $3.90 per trip on a discount pass bought in a 25-trip minimum, and $780 for an annual unlimited pass for one car. Rates can change, so check the authority's current sheet.

How expensive are homes in Boca Grande?

The Census Bureau's 2020-2024 survey puts the median owner-reported home value in ZIP 33921 in its top bracket, $2 million or more. Condos and older cottages can trade below that, so look at current sales for the exact street and building.

Do many Boca Grande owners live there year round?

Fewer than in most local ZIP codes. In ZIP 33921, the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 survey counts about two out of three housing units as held for seasonal or occasional use.

What school is in Boca Grande?

The Island School, a Lee County public charter school in Boca Grande, earned an A from the Florida Department of Education for 2025-26. Confirm grade levels and enrollment rules directly with the school.
