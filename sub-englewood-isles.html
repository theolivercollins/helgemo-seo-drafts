Explore · Englewood · Englewood Isles

Englewood Isles is a canal neighborhood on Lemon Bay, established in 1971, where many homes have a dock out back and a short run to the Gulf.

It sits on the Sarasota side of Englewood, a few minutes from Manasota Beach. Here is what the homeowners association and the county record show. For the wider area, see Explore Englewood and Manasota Key.

The short version

Boating first. Waterways lead to Lemon Bay and the Gulf.

Docks and a marina. Many private docks and lifts, plus a residents' marina.

Deed restricted. Established in 1971, with underground utilities.

Pools are common. About 85 percent of single-family homes have one.

On the water

Boating from Englewood Isles

Canals to Lemon Bay, then the Gulf.

The association says many homes are built along waterways that run directly into Lemon Bay and then to the Gulf of Mexico. Many have private docks and lifts, and residents without one can dock and service boats at the community marina. Some properties sit within a bird sanctuary, and residents report bald eagles, dolphins, and manatees nearby.

Before you buy a canal home, check the seawall, dock, and lift, and ask about water depth at low tide on the route out. Our waterfront buying guide walks through each check.

Homes

Englewood Isles homes for sale

Mostly single-family ranch homes, plus a few condos.

Across the Englewood Isles plats, the Sarasota County roll lists 563 single-family homes and 55 condos. Homes date from 1970 to 2024, with a median build year of 1983, and the median single-family home has about 1,998 square feet.

FAQs

Englewood Isles: common questions

Where is Englewood Isles?

Englewood Isles is on the Sarasota County side of Englewood, ZIP 34223, on the shores of Lemon Bay between Englewood and Venice. The homeowners association says it is about a five-minute drive to Manasota Beach.

Do Englewood Isles homes have boat access?

Many do. The association says a lot of homes sit on waterways that run into Lemon Bay and on to the Gulf, many have private docks and lifts, and residents without a dock can use the community marina.

How many homes are in Englewood Isles?

The Englewood Isles 1 and 2 association describes a 184-home neighborhood. The Sarasota County roll shows about 618 homes across all the Englewood Isles plats, units 1 through 6, including 563 single-family homes.

Is Englewood Isles deed restricted?

Yes. The homeowners association describes it as a deed-restricted community, established in 1971, with public water and sewer and underground utilities.

What do homes in Englewood Isles cost?

The county's median just value for a single-family home across the Englewood Isles plats is about $345,000. The 45 qualified single-family sales recorded in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $500,000.
