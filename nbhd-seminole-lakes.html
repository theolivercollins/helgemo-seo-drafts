Explore · Punta Gorda · Seminole Lakes

Seminole Lakes is a gated Punta Gorda neighborhood of about 450 homes wrapped around eight lakes and an executive golf course, all inside city limits.

Development began in 1992. Today the community is known for its lakes, its active social calendar, and a golf course run by a separate club. If you like the idea of Punta Gorda but want a gate, lakes instead of canals, and a smaller community feel, Seminole Lakes belongs on your list. Location

Where Seminole Lakes is

A gated pocket south of downtown Punta Gorda.

Seminole Lakes is a gated community within Punta Gorda city limits, south of downtown in ZIP 33955. The golf club sits at 26200 Stillwater Circle. Being inside the city means City of Punta Gorda services and a city property tax line on the bill, in addition to county taxes.

From here, downtown Punta Gorda, the Harborwalk, and Fishermen's Village are a short drive north, and Burnt Store Road and the Burnt Store Marina area are to the south and west. Our list of things to do in Punta Gorda covers what is close by.

Source: Seminole Lakes POA.

Golf

The Seminole Lakes golf course

Short, quick, and separate from the HOA.

Seminole Lakes Golf and Country Club is an 18-hole, par-62 executive course of about 4,400 yards, designed by Ron Garl and established in 1992. It is semi-private, open year round, and operates as a separate organization from the property owners association.

Homes

Seminole Lakes homes for sale

Homes from the 1990s onward.

Seminole Lakes homes have been built since development began in 1992, and many sit on a lake or the golf course. Because the homes span three decades, condition varies widely, so roofs, air conditioning, windows, and storm protection matter as much as floor plan.

For context, ZIP 33955, which includes Seminole Lakes, Burnt Store Marina, and other south Punta Gorda neighborhoods, has a median owner-reported home value of $376,200 in the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 survey, and about 44 percent of its homes were built in 2000 or later. Those are ZIP-wide numbers, not Seminole Lakes prices, so look at recent sales inside the gate.

FAQs

Seminole Lakes: common questions

Where is Seminole Lakes in Punta Gorda?

Seminole Lakes is a gated community inside Punta Gorda city limits, south of downtown in ZIP 33955. The golf club's address is 26200 Stillwater Circle, Punta Gorda, FL 33955.

How many homes are in Seminole Lakes?

The Seminole Lakes Property Owners Association says the community has 450 homes and 8 lakes. Development began in 1992.

Is the Seminole Lakes golf course part of the HOA?

No. Seminole Lakes Golf and Country Club is a separate organization from the property owners association. The course is semi-private, so ask the club directly about membership and fees.

What kind of golf course is Seminole Lakes?

It is an 18-hole, par-62 executive course of about 4,400 yards, designed by Ron Garl and established in 1992, according to the club.

What amenities does Seminole Lakes have?

The property owners association lists bocce courts, a heated pool, a workout room, a library, and a tiki hut. The separate golf club has a pro shop, practice greens, and a clubhouse.

Is Seminole Lakes in the city of Punta Gorda?

Yes. The association says Seminole Lakes is within Punta Gorda city limits, so homes pay City of Punta Gorda property taxes along with county taxes.
