The basics

A Sarasota County city next door

Different county, different offices.

North Port is an incorporated city in southern Sarasota County, bordering Charlotte County. Its ACS 2020-2024 median household income is $84,049 and 80.9 percent of occupied homes are owner-occupied. Property records and exemptions go through the Sarasota County Property Appraiser, not Charlotte County's.

Look up any North Port parcel on the Sarasota County Property Appraiser site. For Charlotte County homes, use our Charlotte County Property Appraiser guide.

FAQs

North Port homes: common questions

What county is North Port, FL in?

Sarasota County. North Port is an incorporated city in southern Sarasota County, next to Charlotte County, so property records are with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser.

What is the median home value in North Port?

The ACS 2020-2024 median owner-reported value for North Port is $362,500. It is a survey estimate, not a sale price, so ask for recent comparable sales.

Are most North Port homes new?

Newer than nearby areas. In ACS 2020-2024 data, 69.5 percent of North Port's housing units were built in 2000 or later, compared with 16.6 percent in Port Charlotte.

Are North Port schools good?

Sarasota County Schools earned an A for 2025-26. In North Port, Toledo Blade, Atwater, Cranberry, and Glenallen elementary schools, Heron Creek and Woodland middle schools, and Imagine School at North Port earned an A, and North Port High earned a B.

How many people live in North Port?

84,605 people, according to ACS 2020-2024 estimates, with a median age of 49.3.
