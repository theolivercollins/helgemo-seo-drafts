Explore · Englewood · Alameda Isles

Alameda Isles is a gated 55+ co-op of 355 manufactured homes on 65 waterfront acres, with its own 58-slip marina that reaches the Gulf.

The residents bought the park themselves in a court fight in the late 1980s, and they still own it today. Here is what the community and the county record show. For the wider area, see Explore Englewood.

History

How the residents bought the park

A 1975 park that became a co-op.

Alameda Isles opened in 1975 on about 64.5 acres along a canal that later became the marina, with 355 rented home sites. When the owner ran into financial trouble in 1987 and put the park up for sale, the homeowners association used a new Florida law that gave manufactured home park residents a right of first refusal. A federal bankruptcy judge ruled the residents could buy the park by matching any offer. They matched the only offer, $6.305 million, raised $2.5 million in cash within a week, and made the final payment on May 8, 1996.

Source: Alameda Isles, about.

Amenities

Life in Alameda Isles

Marina, clubhouse, and a busy calendar.

The community lists a heated pool and spa, an exercise room, and courts for tennis, bocce, shuffleboard, and pickleball. The private park has grills, picnic tables, a putting green, and a driving cage. The clubhouse has an event hall with a kitchen and stage, a library and lounge, craft and billiard rooms, a sauna, and an on-site management office. There is also a woodworking and metal shop and an RV and boat storage yard.

Clubs include a boat club, veterans club, and women's club, with leagues for bowling, golf, tennis, pickleball, shuffleboard, and bocce.

FAQs

Alameda Isles: common questions

Where is Alameda Isles?

Alameda Isles is on the Sarasota County side of Englewood, ZIP 34223. Its marina connects to Lemon Bay, the Intracoastal Waterway, and the Gulf.

Is Alameda Isles 55 and over?

Yes. The community describes itself as a 55 or older cooperative association that is resident owned and gated.

Does Alameda Isles have boat slips?

Yes. The community has a private 58-slip marina with Intracoastal and Gulf access. Slips are leased based on availability, as is space in the RV and boat storage yard.

How did residents come to own Alameda Isles?

The park opened in 1975 with rented home sites. When the owner put it up for sale in the late 1980s, residents used Florida's right of first refusal for manufactured home park residents, won in bankruptcy court, matched the only offer, and made the final payment on May 8, 1996.

What do homes in Alameda Isles cost?

The county's median just value for a home here is about $120,000. The 31 qualified sales recorded in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $175,000.
