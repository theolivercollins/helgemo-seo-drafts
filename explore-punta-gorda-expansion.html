Living in Punta Gorda, FL

Punta Gorda is a small harbor city with a big range of homes: walkable downtown streets, miles of canals, marina villages, and quiet inland neighborhoods.

Punta Gorda is the county seat and only incorporated city in Charlotte County, at the mouth of the Peace River on Charlotte Harbor. ZIP codes

Punta Gorda ZIP codes

A Punta Gorda address is not always a city address.

Punta Gorda uses four residential ZIP codes: 33950 (downtown, Punta Gorda Isles, Burnt Store Isles), 33955 (south along US 41 and Burnt Store Road, including Burnt Store Marina), 33982 (east county, including Babcock Ranch), and 33983 (Deep Creek). Only 33950 and 33955 overlap the city limits in Census Bureau data.

Schools

Schools serving Punta Gorda

Graded by the state for 2025-26.

Charlotte County Public Schools earned an A for 2025-26. Schools with Punta Gorda and Deep Creek addresses include Sallie Jones Elementary (A), Deep Creek Elementary (A), East Elementary (B), Punta Gorda Middle (A), and Charlotte High School (B). Florida SouthWestern Collegiate High, a charter school in Punta Gorda, earned an A.

Schools are assigned by attendance boundary, so check the exact address. See every grade in our Charlotte County schools guide.

Before you buy

Four things to settle before you write an offer

Water and weather drive the big costs here.

Before buying in Punta Gorda, check the FEMA flood zone and get an insurance quote, confirm whether the home is inside the city limits, review canal, seawall, and association costs, and plan your homestead filing if it will be your permanent residence. Hurricane Charley in 2004 and Hurricane Ian in 2022 both came ashore in this area.

Living here

Everyday life in Punta Gorda

Harbor town, warm winters, wet summers.

The 2.5-mile Harborwalk connects Laishley Park, Gilchrist Park, and Fishermen's Village along the water. NOAA's 1991-2020 normals show a January average high of 75.3 degrees and a July high of 92.5, with 52.97 inches of rain a year. Punta Gorda Airport served a record 2,282,002 passengers in 2025.

FAQs

Punta Gorda homes: common questions

Is Punta Gorda a city?

Yes. Punta Gorda is the county seat and the only incorporated city in Charlotte County, with 20,278 residents in ACS 2020-2024 data. Many homes with a Punta Gorda mailing address are outside the city limits, in unincorporated Charlotte County.

What are the zip codes for Punta Gorda, FL?

33950, 33955, 33982, and 33983, plus 33951 for post office boxes. 33950 covers downtown and Punta Gorda Isles.

What is the median home value in Punta Gorda?

The ACS 2020-2024 median owner-reported value for the city is $518,100, against $328,900 for Charlotte County. That is a survey estimate, not a sale price, so ask for recent comparable sales.

Does Punta Gorda have waterfront homes?

Yes. Punta Gorda Isles has about 45 miles of canals and Burnt Store Isles about 9 miles, and the city maintains the seawalls through assessment districts. Check boat access, bridges, and the seawall for any specific home.

Are the schools good in Punta Gorda?

Charlotte County Public Schools earned an A for 2025-26. Sallie Jones Elementary, Deep Creek Elementary, and Punta Gorda Middle earned an A, and Charlotte High School and East Elementary earned a B.
