Burnt Store Lakes is a deed-restricted lake community off Burnt Store Road, south of Punta Gorda, where about half the lots are still empty and new homes go up every year.

It is recorded as Punta Gorda Isles Section 21, but it feels nothing like the canal sections of Punta Gorda Isles. Think lakes, greenbelts, and room to build, next door to Burnt Store Marina. Here is what to know about Burnt Store Lakes homes and lots for sale.

Location

Where Burnt Store Lakes is

Between Punta Gorda and Cape Coral.

Burnt Store Lakes sits about nine miles south down Burnt Store Road from U.S. 41. Its property owners association says it is bordered to the north by Punta Gorda, to the south by Burnt Store Marina and Cape Coral, and to the west by the Charlotte Harbor Buffer Preserve. Addresses are in ZIP 33955; Cape Horn Boulevard is the main street.

The association office is at 16500 Burnt Store Road, Unit 102. From here, Burnt Store Marina's restaurants and golf are minutes south, and downtown Punta Gorda is a short drive north.

Homes and lots

What you can buy in Burnt Store Lakes

Lots, new builds, and 2000s homes.

The county roll shows 745 single-family homes, 951 vacant residential lots, and about 140 more parcels carrying a new-construction code in Section 21. The median home was built in 2006 with about 2,166 square feet of living area, and 74 percent of homes have a pool.

Community

Lakes, park, and the POA

A self-run, low-cost association.

Every lot in Burnt Store Lakes belongs to the Burnt Store Lakes Property Owners Association. The association's website describes freshwater lakes, greenbelts, a community park, and a canoe and kayak launch. Recent listings describe the annual assessment as low compared with most planned communities; get the current figure from the association.

Because Burnt Store Lakes is next to the Charlotte Harbor Buffer Preserve, wildlife and open land are part of daily life. That also means some lots have environmental conditions to check before you build.

Source: Burnt Store Lakes POA.

FAQs

Burnt Store Lakes: common questions

Where is Burnt Store Lakes?

Burnt Store Lakes is about nine miles south down Burnt Store Road from U.S. 41. Its property owners association says it is bordered by Punta Gorda to the north, Burnt Store Marina and Cape Coral to the south, and the Charlotte Harbor Buffer Preserve to the west.

Is Burnt Store Lakes the same as Burnt Store Marina?

No. Burnt Store Lakes is recorded in Charlotte County as Punta Gorda Isles Section 21 and has its own property owners association. Burnt Store Marina is a separate community to the south.

Is Burnt Store Lakes in the city of Punta Gorda?

It has a Punta Gorda mailing address (ZIP 33955), but its association describes the City of Punta Gorda as its northern neighbor. Check the tax bill for any parcel to see which taxing districts apply.

Can you build a new home in Burnt Store Lakes?

Yes. About half of the 1,848 parcels in Section 21 are vacant residential lots on the county roll, and new homes are going up. Lots must follow the community's deed restrictions and architectural review.

How much are lots in Burnt Store Lakes?

The median qualified 2025 and 2026 sale of a vacant residential lot was about $33,500 across 59 sales on the county file. Lot size, water frontage, and utilities change the price.

Is there water access in Burnt Store Lakes?

The community is built around freshwater lakes. Its association website describes a canoe and kayak launch for paddling toward Charlotte Harbor. It is not a deep-water canal community for large boats.
