Deep Creek is a deed-restricted Punta Gorda neighborhood where every lot backs onto green space, and the first thing to learn is which of its two associations your home is in.

Deep Creek sits north of the Peace River near Interstate 75, with Punta Gorda addresses in ZIP 33983. It is known for its greenbelts, freshwater lakes and canals, a public golf course, and a strong volunteer civic association that has served the community since 1985. The two sections

Section 20 and Section 23

One community, two sets of rules.

Sandhill Boulevard divides Deep Creek into two sections. Properties north of Sandhill are generally in Section 20, and properties south are in Section 23. Each property owners association handles its own deed restrictions, common areas, and annual assessments, while the Deep Creek Civic Association handles social events and programs.

Deed restrictions

What the deed restrictions cover

Read them before you plan a fence or a pool.

Deep Creek's declarations cover how homes and lots can be used and changed. Section 23's declaration includes articles on residential use, business use, setbacks, minimum square footage, garages and driveways, pools, landscaping, fences and enclosures, signs, parking, trash, pets, and enforcement. Section 20 requires permits for items such as fences, patios, pavers, and exterior changes.

Daily life

Golf, civic life, and schools

A neighborhood with a calendar.

Deep Creek Golf Club is a semi-private, 18-hole, par-70 course of more than 6,000 yards designed by Mark McCumber, open to the public for a daily fee and located about 2 miles off I-75. The Deep Creek Civic Association runs events from bingo nights to holiday parties, and Deep Creek Elementary School earned an A for 2025-26.

FAQs

Deep Creek: common questions

Where is Deep Creek in Punta Gorda?

Deep Creek is a deed-restricted community in Charlotte County with Punta Gorda addresses in ZIP 33983. Deep Creek Golf Club, at 1260 San Cristobal Avenue, is about 2 miles off Interstate 75, according to the club.

Is Deep Creek deed restricted?

Yes. Deep Creek is deed restricted, and the restrictions are handled by two property owners associations: Section 20, north of Sandhill Boulevard, and Section 23, south of it. Each has its own declaration of restrictions and permits.

What is the difference between Section 20 and Section 23?

Sandhill Boulevard divides them. Properties north of Sandhill are generally in Section 20, and properties south are in Section 23. Each association has its own rules, permits, and annual assessments, so check which one covers a home.

What are the greenbelts in Deep Creek?

Deep Creek was laid out so that lots border open space called greenbelts, which the Section 20 association maintains on its side. Many homes also sit on freshwater lakes or canals.

Is there a golf course in Deep Creek?

Yes. Deep Creek Golf Club is a semi-private, 18-hole, par-70 course of more than 6,000 yards designed by Mark McCumber, open to the public for a daily fee, according to the club.

How much are homes in Deep Creek?

For ZIP 33983, which includes Deep Creek, the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 survey puts the median owner-reported home value at $363,400. Lake and canal lots and newer homes trade higher, so compare recent sales in the same section.
