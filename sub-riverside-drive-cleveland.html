Explore · Punta Gorda · Riverside Drive

Riverside Drive is a neighborhood of its own: a quiet road along the Peace River where 1880s town history, older cottages, mobile home parks, and new river homes sit side by side.

It feels nothing like the canal grid of Punta Gorda Isles. Lots are irregular, many homes are older, and the river is the main event. Here is the history, what the county record shows, and what to check before you buy. For the city as a whole, see Explore Punta Gorda.

History

Cleveland, the town on the river

A Grover Cleveland namesake with a 1920s charter.

In 1885 Dr. Alfred T. Holleyman, a sportsman from New York, platted a town here and named it for the newly elected President Grover Cleveland. He built the Baxter House, a two-story hotel at the foot of Cleveland Avenue that held 40 guests. The Florida Southern Railway reached Cleveland in May 1886. During the 1920s land boom the town incorporated to pay for street improvements, and curbing from that era still survives where Cleveland Avenue meets Riverside Drive. When the Depression hit, the town defaulted on its bonds and voided its charter. The old hotel later became the Peace River Lodge and burned in 1965.

The area was also the site of Camp Whipple, a temporary Army outpost established in January 1857 during the Third Seminole War.

Source: Charlotte Sun and the Charlotte County Historical Center Society, "The history of Cleveland"; USGS Geographic Names Information System.

Pockets

The pockets along Riverside Drive

Several old plats share one road.

Locals talk about Riverside Drive as one place, but the county record splits it into several plats. Cleveland North and Cleveland South hold the original town grid near Cleveland Avenue. Riverside Park is a separate plat along the road. McKinley Park mixes manufactured homes and houses, and River Forest is a mobile home co-op. Shell Creek Heights sits farther east, and Rivers Edge is a handful of homes built in 2023. Unplatted acreage fills the gaps.

Homes

Riverside Drive homes for sale

From cottages to river estates.

Across the corridor, single-family homes were built from 1920 to 2026, with a median of 1973, and 57 percent predate 1980. The median home has about 1,530 square feet of living area. On Riverside Drive itself, about 50 percent of homes carry the county's waterfront flag, and those river lots drive the high end of the market.

FAQs

Riverside Drive and Cleveland: common questions

Where is Riverside Drive in Punta Gorda?

Riverside Drive runs along the south side of the Peace River just north and east of downtown Punta Gorda, in ZIP 33982, unincorporated Charlotte County. The historic Cleveland area sits between Riverside Drive and U.S. 17.

Was Cleveland, Florida once a town?

Yes. Cleveland was platted in 1885 and named for President Grover Cleveland. It incorporated during the 1920s land boom, then voided its charter after defaulting on its bonds in the Great Depression, according to the Charlotte County Historical Center Society's history column.

What kinds of homes are on Riverside Drive?

A mix. The corridor has about 184 single-family homes, 50 individually owned mobile homes, and the 204-unit River Forest mobile home co-op, plus 92 vacant lots. Homes date from 1920 to 2026.

What do homes on Riverside Drive cost?

Prices swing widely. The middle half of county just values for single-family homes runs from about $104,000 to $434,000. Riverfront homes sell far higher: the 12 qualified single-family sales in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $858,000.

Is Riverside Drive in the City of Punta Gorda?

No. The corridor uses Punta Gorda mailing addresses but sits in unincorporated Charlotte County, so county rules and services apply.
