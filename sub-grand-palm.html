Explore · Venice · Grand Palm

Grand Palm is a newer community of about 1,732 homes southeast of Venice, built around lakes, trails, and a big resident amenity center.

It is one of the neighborhoods in the Wellen Park family, and every home here dates from 2007 or later. Here is what Wellen Park and the county record show. For the wider area, see Explore Venice.

Amenities

Life in Grand Palm

Built around the outdoors.

Wellen Park describes Grand Palm as a nature-focused community. The amenity list includes a residents social club with an outdoor social area, a fitness center, a resort-style pool with water slides, and Splash Down Park. Adventure Island is a private park with walking trails, a pavilion, picnic tables, and a kayak launch.

Homes

Grand Palm homes for sale

Mostly single-family, with a villa section.

The Sarasota County roll lists 1,260 single-family homes and 472 attached villas across the Grand Palm phases. The median single-family home has about 2,289 square feet of living area, and about 52 percent of single-family homes have a pool.

FAQs

Grand Palm: common questions

Where is Grand Palm?

Grand Palm is in unincorporated Sarasota County with a Venice address, ZIP 34293. Wellen Park lists it among its neighborhoods, and its sales center address is 21209 Wacissa Drive, Venice.

Is Grand Palm part of Wellen Park?

Yes. Wellen Park, the master-planned community southeast of Venice, lists Grand Palm as one of its neighborhoods.

What amenities does Grand Palm have?

Wellen Park lists a residents social club, a fitness center, a resort-style pool with water slides, Splash Down Park, and Adventure Island, a private park with walking trails, a pavilion, picnic tables, and a kayak launch.

How many homes are in Grand Palm?

The Sarasota County parcel roll shows about 1,732 homes in the Grand Palm plats: 1,260 single-family homes and 472 attached villas, built from 2007 to 2026.

What do homes in Grand Palm cost?

The county's median just value for a Grand Palm single-family home is about $472,000. The 136 qualified single-family sales recorded in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $640,000.
