Venice, FL Neighborhoods: The Island, the Golf Communities, and How the ZIP Codes Differ

Explore · Venice, Florida

Venice is a planned 1920s city on the Gulf, and the neighborhood you pick decides whether you live near the historic downtown, on a golf course behind a gate, or in a 1960s ranch street a few minutes from the beach.

The City of Venice covers the island and a stretch of mainland, but the Venice name reaches well past the city line into South Venice, Venice Gardens, and the newer communities to the east and southeast. Here is how the pieces fit, with Census figures, county parcel data, and links to every Venice neighborhood guide we have.

History

How Venice was planned

A 1920s city that was finished after the war.

The area went by Horse and Chaise before a post office named it Venice in 1888. In the Florida land boom of the 1920s, orthopedic surgeon Dr. Fred H. Albee bought 112 acres from Bertha Palmer and hired city planner John Nolen. Albee soon sold the land to the Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers, which kept Nolen on and had him design the walkable, mixed-use town that is still the historic heart of Venice. Venice became a town on July 1, 1926, and a city on May 9, 1927.

The boom ended before the plan was built out. Its major elements were completed after World War II, in 1960, and the John Nolen Plan of Venice Historic District was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2010. The city counts eight individually listed properties and five national historic districts, including Venezia Park, Edgewood, and the Armada Road Multi-Family District.

Source: City of Venice, John Nolen and His Plan of Venice; City of Venice, National Register of Historic Places in Venice; Wikipedia, Venice, Florida.

ZIP codes

Venice by ZIP code

Three ZIP codes, three different housing stocks.

The Census numbers show the split. ZIP 34285, the island and older city, has the oldest homes and the most seasonal units. ZIP 34292 to the east is mostly built since 2000. ZIP 34293, the largest by population, mixes 1960s and 1970s South Venice and Venice Gardens with the newer communities southeast toward Wellen Park.

Beaches

The beaches and the shark teeth

Why people dig in the sand here.

Venice has been called the Shark's Tooth Capital of the World. Fossilized shark teeth wash up along its Gulf beaches, and the city hosts an annual Shark's Tooth Festival to celebrate them. Bring a sifter and look in the shell line at the water's edge.

Source: Wikipedia, Venice, Florida, arts and culture.

Schools

Schools serving Venice

A-rated public schools on the 2025-26 report.

Venice is in the Sarasota County school district. Venice Elementary, Venice Middle, and Venice Senior High all earned A grades from the Florida Department of Education for 2025-26, as did Garden Elementary, Taylor Ranch Elementary, and Laurel Nokomis School.

Storms

Hurricanes, flood zones, and insurance

Venice took surge in 2024.

Hurricane Helene pushed several feet of storm surge through the City of Venice in September 2024. Less than two weeks later, on October 9, 2024, Hurricane Milton made landfall just north of Venice near Siesta Key. FAQs

Venice: common questions

What county is Venice, Florida in?

Venice is in Sarasota County. The City of Venice has its own city government, but many homes with a Venice address, including South Venice, Venice Gardens, and much of the golf community belt, are in unincorporated Sarasota County. Check the parcel's city on the property appraiser record.

What is Venice Island?

Venice Island is the part of the city between the Gulf and the Intracoastal Waterway, reached by bridges. It holds the historic downtown laid out under the John Nolen plan and the city's Gulf beaches.

How much are homes in Venice, FL?

The Census Bureau's 2020-2024 survey puts the median owner-reported home value at $435K in the City of Venice, $337K in South Venice, and $347K in Venice Gardens. On the county roll, the median just value of a single-family home inside the city is about $422,000.

Is Venice, FL mostly retirees?

Venice skews older than almost anywhere in Florida. The median age in the city is 69.2 in the 2020-2024 survey, and the 2020 census found 62.8 percent of residents were 65 or older.

What ZIP codes are in Venice, FL?

Venice addresses use 34285, 34292, and 34293. ZIP 34285 covers the island and the older city, 34292 runs east toward Jacaranda Boulevard and the golf communities, and 34293 covers South Venice, Venice Gardens, and the newer communities to the southeast. Some newer city neighborhoods off Laurel Road carry a Nokomis 34275 address.

Why is Venice called the Shark's Tooth Capital of the World?

Venice has long been called that because fossilized shark teeth wash up on its beaches. The city also hosts an annual Shark's Tooth Festival.
