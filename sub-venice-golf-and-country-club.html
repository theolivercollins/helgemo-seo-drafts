Membership

How membership works

The house and the club come together.

The club says a membership purchase is required to live in the community. Homeowners choose either a Golf membership or a Club membership, and non-resident memberships are not available. Club membership includes unlimited access to the clubhouse, wellness center, tennis, aquatics, and dining, and the community is golf cart friendly.

Owners contribute each year to both the master association and the club. There are no community development district fees. Ask the membership office for the current equity, dues, and any waiting list before you make an offer.

FAQs

Venice Golf and Country Club: common questions

Where is Venice Golf and Country Club?

The club is at 250 Venice Golf Club Drive, Venice, FL 34292, in unincorporated Sarasota County.

Is membership required at Venice Golf and Country Club?

Yes. The club says a membership purchase is required to live in the community, and every homeowner chooses either a Golf or a Club membership. Non-resident memberships are not offered.

Does Venice Golf and Country Club have a CDD?

No. The club says there are no community development district fees. Owners pay the master association and club membership each year.

Is the community gated?

Yes. Residents enter through a 24-hour manned gatehouse.

What does the Club membership include?

The club says Club membership includes unlimited access to the clubhouse, wellness center, tennis, aquatics, and dining. Golf membership adds the course.

What do homes in Venice Golf and Country Club cost?

The county's median just value for a single-family home here is about $491,000. The 43 qualified single-family sales recorded in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $685,000.
