Burnt Store Marina Homes: A Local Guide

By Oliver HelgemoCommunityNovember 10, 2026

Burnt Store Marina, Punta Gorda & Charlotte Harbor, FL

Quick Answer

Burnt Store Marina is a marina community on the Lee County side of the line.

Burnt Store Marina sits on Charlotte Harbor just south of the Charlotte County line, with a Punta Gorda ZIP code. About 43.5 percent of its housing units are in buildings with five or more units, so you will compare villas, condominiums, and single-family homes, and you will want to understand the slip before you buy.

If you are searching for Burnt Store Marina homes for sale, start with the home type and the slip, then look at the community.

What Burnt Store Marina actually is

Burnt Store Marina is an unincorporated community in northwestern Lee County. It is bordered to the south by Cape Coral and to the north by Charlotte County, its west side fronts Charlotte Harbor, and its east side follows Burnt Store Road. It is about 13 miles south of Punta Gorda. The postal address is Punta Gorda, and the ZIP code is 33955, so a search for Punta Gorda homes often returns Burnt Store Marina listings even though the community is not inside the city or the county.

That matters for the paperwork. The Lee County Property Appraiser, not Charlotte County, handles homestead and property records, and Lee County rules apply to building and zoning in unincorporated areas. Ask the county for the rules on the specific parcel. Our Burnt Store Marina area page covers the neighborhood, and the Punta Gorda real estate guide explains how it fits with the city's canal neighborhoods, Burnt Store Isles, and Burnt Store Meadows.

Villas, condominiums, and single-family homes

The housing mix is what sets this community apart. In the ACS 2020-2024 data, 43.5 percent of units are in buildings with five or more units, compared with 13.0 percent for Charlotte County, and another 10.1 percent are attached single-family homes. Only about a third are detached single-family houses. That means the typical Burnt Store Marina search involves villas and multi-unit buildings, and that means the condominium questions in our guide to Punta Gorda condos apply here.

For any building of three or more habitable stories, ask for the milestone inspection report if one applies, the Structural Integrity Reserve Study, the budget, the reserves, and any special assessments. For villas and townhome-style homes, find out what the association maintains, such as roofs, exteriors, landscaping, and insurance, and what it does not. A detached single-family home on a canal or fairway is a different purchase, and the lot and seawall become your own checks.

The marina, the slip, and the water

The marina is the heart of the community. Its operator advertises wet slips, transient slips, and dry storage on Charlotte Harbor. Do not assume a home comes with a slip. Slips may be owned, leased, or sold separately, and the terms, fees, and transfer rules sit in separate documents. Ask for the slip size, the depth, the fee schedule, the waitlist, and whether it can transfer to a buyer.

Also ask about the route. Charlotte Harbor is open water with shallow areas, so a deeper-draft boat needs a plan for the channel. Verify depths and clearances for your boat, and test the trip before you rely on it. If you are comparing marina living with canal-front homes, read our guides to Punta Gorda Isles and waterfront buying for the differences.

Golf, amenities, and seasonal living

The country club lists a 27-hole executive course, so ask what golf and club access, if any, comes with a particular home. As with Rotonda West, a course view is not a golf membership. Because 28.8 percent of units are seasonal and the median age is 72.8, the community runs on a seasonal rhythm. That affects how quiet it is in summer, how fast homes move, and what you need to do to prepare an unoccupied house for storms.

Think about whether that rhythm fits how you plan to use the home. The fit depends on how you will use the water, the club, and the house, not on a stereotype about who lives here.

Buying at Burnt Store Marina: a few local realities

Insurance and flood zones belong in the first conversation. FEMA's Risk Rating 2.0 prices flood insurance using factors such as distance to flooding sources, ground elevation, first-floor height, and foundation type. Ask for the elevation certificate, and get quotes for both the unit or house and the building.

Storm history. Hurricane Ian made a second landfall near Punta Gorda on September 28, 2022, according to the National Hurricane Center's final report, and Charley came ashore in the same area in 2004. Ask each association about its storm history and its repairs, and ask the seller about the dock, lift, and seawall.

Homestead. If the home will be your primary residence, ask the Lee County Property Appraiser about Florida's homestead exemption, up to $50,000 of assessed value, with the first $25,000 applying to all property taxes. Save Our Homes limits annual increases to the lesser of 3 percent or the change in the Consumer Price Index, which is 2.7 percent for 2026. You must own and live in the home on January 1 and apply by March 1.

Price context. The FHFA house-price index for the Punta Gorda metro area was 391.34 in the second quarter of 2026, down 0.2 percent from a year earlier and 7.7 percent below the second quarter of 2024. That index covers Charlotte County, not the Lee County side where the marina sits, so it is only a regional reference. Ask for recent comparable sales for the same home type and slip situation. Frequently asked questions

Is Burnt Store Marina in Punta Gorda?

It has a Punta Gorda ZIP code (33955), but it is an unincorporated community in Lee County, just south of the Charlotte County line and about 13 miles south of the city. Check jurisdiction, property appraiser, and services for any specific address.

What types of homes are at Burnt Store Marina?

A mix. In ACS 2020-2024 data, 43.5 percent of housing units are in buildings with five or more units, 10.1 percent are attached single-family homes, and 32.8 percent are detached single-family homes.

Does a home at Burnt Store Marina come with a boat slip?

Not necessarily. Slips may be owned, leased, or sold separately, and fees and transfer terms vary. Ask for the slip documents before you make an offer.

Is there a golf course at Burnt Store Marina?

Yes. Burnt Store Marina Country Club lists a 27-hole executive course. Ask what access and membership come with a particular home.

How seasonal is Burnt Store Marina?

In ACS 2020-2024 data, 28.8 percent of housing units are held for seasonal, recreational, or occasional use, compared with 15.7 percent for Charlotte County.

What is the median home value at Burnt Store Marina?

The Census Bureau's ACS 2020-2024 estimate for the median owner-reported value is $430,100, with a margin of error of about $49,000. It is a survey estimate, not a median sale price, and it covers all housing types.

Is Burnt Store Marina the same as Burnt Store Isles?

No. Burnt Store Isles is a canal district inside the city of Punta Gorda, with about 9 miles of canals according to the city. Burnt Store Marina is a separate community on Charlotte Harbor in Lee County.
