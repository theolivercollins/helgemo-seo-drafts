Burnt Store Marina Homes: A Local Guide

By Brian HelgemoCommunityNovember 10, 2026

Burnt Store Marina, Punta Gorda & Charlotte Harbor, FL

Burnt Store Marina has a Punta Gorda ZIP code, a Charlotte Harbor address, and a Lee County tax bill. Most of the confusion starts right there.

Burnt Store Marina is an unincorporated community in northwestern Lee County, about 13 miles south of Punta Gorda. Its west side fronts Charlotte Harbor, and its east side follows Burnt Store Road. A search for Punta Gorda homes often returns its listings even though the community is not inside the city or the county.

Part 01

Five assumptions worth testing

Myth on top, fact underneath.

The marina basin at Burnt Store Marina, with villas along the slips.

MYTH 1

"It is in Punta Gorda."

FACT

It has a Punta Gorda ZIP code (33955), but it is an unincorporated community in Lee County, just south of the Charlotte County line. That matters for the paperwork: the Lee County Property Appraiser, not Charlotte County, handles homestead and property records, and Lee County rules apply to building and zoning. Ask the county about the specific parcel.

MYTH 2

"It is mostly single-family houses."

FACT

In the ACS 2020-2024 data, 43.5 percent of housing units are in buildings with five or more units, against 13.0 percent for Charlotte County. Only 32.8 percent are detached single-family homes.

MYTH 3

"A home here comes with a boat slip."

FACT

Not necessarily. Slips may be owned, leased, or sold separately, and the terms, fees, and transfer rules sit in separate documents.

MYTH 4

"Burnt Store Marina and Burnt Store Isles are the same place."

FACT

They are not. Burnt Store Isles is a canal district inside the city of Punta Gorda with about 9 miles of canals, according to the city. Burnt Store Marina is a separate community on Charlotte Harbor in Lee County.

MYTH 5

"A golf course view means golf access."

FACT

Burnt Store Marina Country Club lists a 27-hole executive course. As with any community, a course view is not a membership. Ask what golf and club access, if any, comes with a particular home.

Source: City of Punta Gorda, Canals; Burnt Store Marina Country Club; Wikipedia, Burnt Store Marina.

Part 03

The marina, the slip, and the water

The marina is the heart of the community, and the slip is its own purchase.

The marina's operator advertises wet slips, transient slips, and dry storage on Charlotte Harbor. Do not assume a home comes with a slip. Before you make an offer, get these in writing.

Slip checklist

Slip questions to ask

Size and depth. What boat length and draft does the slip handle?

Fee schedule. What are the current dock fees and any increases?

Waitlist. Is there one, and where would you be on it?

Transfer. Can the slip transfer to a buyer, and on what terms?

The route. Charlotte Harbor is open water with shallow areas, so a deeper-draft boat needs a plan for the channel. Test the trip.

02

Storm history and homestead

Hurricane Ian made a second landfall near Punta Gorda on September 28, 2022, and Charley came ashore in the same area in 2004. Ask each association about its storm history and repairs, and ask the seller about the dock, lift, and seawall. If the home will be your primary residence, ask the Lee County Property Appraiser about Florida's homestead exemption: up to $50,000 of assessed value, with the first $25,000 applying to all property taxes. Save Our Homes limits annual assessed-value increases to the lesser of 3 percent or the change in the Consumer Price Index, which is 2.7 percent for 2026. You must own and live in the home as your permanent residence on January 1 and apply by March 1.

Part 06

Burnt Store Marina questions, answered

Is Burnt Store Marina in Punta Gorda?

It has a Punta Gorda ZIP code (33955), but it is an unincorporated community in Lee County, just south of the Charlotte County line and about 13 miles south of the city. Check jurisdiction, property appraiser, and services for any specific address.

What types of homes are at Burnt Store Marina?

A mix. In ACS 2020-2024 data, 43.5 percent of housing units are in buildings with five or more units, 10.1 percent are attached single-family homes, and 32.8 percent are detached single-family homes.

Does a home at Burnt Store Marina come with a boat slip?

Not necessarily. Slips may be owned, leased, or sold separately, and fees and transfer terms vary. Ask for the slip documents before you make an offer.

Is there a golf course at Burnt Store Marina?

Yes. Burnt Store Marina Country Club lists a 27-hole executive course. Ask what access and membership come with a particular home.

How seasonal is Burnt Store Marina?

In ACS 2020-2024 data, 28.8 percent of housing units are held for seasonal, recreational, or occasional use, compared with 15.7 percent for Charlotte County.

What is the median home value at Burnt Store Marina?

The Census Bureau's ACS 2020-2024 estimate for the median owner-reported value is $430,100, with a margin of error of about $49,000. It is a survey estimate, not a median sale price, and it covers all housing types.

Is Burnt Store Marina the same as Burnt Store Isles?

No. Burnt Store Isles is a canal district inside the city of Punta Gorda, with about 9 miles of canals according to the city. Burnt Store Marina is a separate community on Charlotte Harbor in Lee County.
