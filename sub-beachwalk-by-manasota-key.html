BeachWalk by Manasota Key, Englewood: A New Community 2.5 Miles from the Beach

Explore · Englewood · BeachWalk by Manasota Key

BeachWalk by Manasota Key is a large new community on the Sarasota County side of Englewood, with new homes about 2.5 miles from the beach.

Homes here are all from the 2020s, and the amenity campus is still growing. This page covers what the county record and the community publish, and what to check before you buy. For the beach itself, see Manasota Key, and for the wider area, Explore Englewood.

Location

Where BeachWalk is

Off Englewood Road in ZIP 34223.

BeachWalk sits off Englewood Road in ZIP 34223, in unincorporated Sarasota County. The association puts it about 2.5 miles from the beach on Manasota Key.

Source: BeachWalk Manasota association site.

Amenities

The amenity campus

Courts, trails, and two restaurants.

The association describes a large amenity campus with numerous sport courts and walking trails, plus two on-site restaurants: High Tide, an outdoor bar and grill, and The Waterline, a sit-down eatery. The community was still being built out in late 2026, so ask what is open and what is planned.

Source: BeachWalk Manasota association site.

Homes

BeachWalk homes for sale

Single-family homes and paired villas.

Sarasota County's roll shows 1,387 parcels on the BeachWalk plats: 780 single-family homes, 160 attached villas, and 433 lots not yet built on. The median home has about 2,075 square feet of living area. Attached villas carry a median just value of about $270,000. Because so many sales are first sales of new homes, resale prices may differ.

FAQs

BeachWalk by Manasota Key: common questions

Where is BeachWalk by Manasota Key?

BeachWalk is off Englewood Road in Englewood, ZIP 34223, in unincorporated Sarasota County. The community says it is about 2.5 miles from the beach on Manasota Key.

How many homes are in BeachWalk?

Sarasota County's roll shows 1,387 parcels on the BeachWalk plats so far, including 780 single-family homes and 433 lots not yet built on.

What fees come with a BeachWalk home?

Homeowners pay association dues. Ask for the current budget and fee schedule, and check the Sarasota County tax bill for any special assessments.

What amenities does BeachWalk have?

The association lists a large amenity campus, numerous sport courts, walking trails, and two on-site restaurants: High Tide, an outdoor bar and grill, and The Waterline, a sit-down eatery.

What do homes in BeachWalk cost?

The median county just value of a single-family home is about $510,000. The 299 qualified single-family sales in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $715,000, mostly first sales of new homes.
