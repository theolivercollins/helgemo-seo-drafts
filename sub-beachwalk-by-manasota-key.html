Explore · Englewood · BeachWalk by Manasota Key

BeachWalk by Manasota Key is a large new community on the Sarasota County side of Englewood: a gated neighborhood of new homes about 2.5 miles from the Gulf.

Homes here are all from the 2020s, and the amenity campus is still growing. This page covers what the county record and the community publish, and what to check before you buy. For the beach itself, see Manasota Key, and for the wider area, Explore Englewood.

Location

Where BeachWalk is

Off Englewood Road in ZIP 34223.

BeachWalk sits off Englewood Road in ZIP 34223, in unincorporated Sarasota County. The community lists Blind Pass Beach on Manasota Key about 2.3 miles away, U.S. 41 about 3 miles away, and everyday shopping within about five minutes. Wellen Park's downtown is about 9 miles north.

Source: BeachWalk by Manasota Key, community sales page.

Amenities

The amenity campus

Resort-style, with more still coming.

The community publishes a saltwater resort pool with lap lanes and a heated spa, a clubhouse with a fitness center, two movement studios, and card rooms, 10 pickleball courts, 5 tennis courts, 6 bocce courts, a lakeside boardwalk with pier and gazebo, a dog park, fire pits, a community garden, and miles of walking trails. The association site lists two on-site restaurants, an outdoor bar and grill and a sit-down eatery. Some pieces were still being built in late 2026, so confirm what is open.

Source: BeachWalk by Manasota Key, community sales page; BeachWalk Manasota association site.

Homes

BeachWalk homes for sale

Single-family homes and paired villas.

Sarasota County's roll shows 1,387 parcels on the BeachWalk plats: 780 single-family homes, 160 attached villas, and 433 lots not yet built on. The median home has about 2,075 square feet of living area. Attached villas carry a median just value of about $270,000. Because so many sales are first sales of new homes, resale prices may differ.

FAQs

BeachWalk by Manasota Key: common questions

Where is BeachWalk by Manasota Key?

BeachWalk is off Englewood Road in Englewood, ZIP 34223, in unincorporated Sarasota County. The community says it is about 2.5 miles from the beach on Manasota Key.

Is BeachWalk gated?

Yes. The community's sales page describes it as gated, with about 1,500 single-family and multi-family homes planned.

Does BeachWalk have a CDD?

The community's sales page lists no CDD fee. Homeowners pay association dues, so ask for the current budget and fee schedule.

What amenities does BeachWalk have?

Published amenities include a saltwater resort pool with lap lanes and spa, a clubhouse with a fitness center and movement studios, 10 pickleball courts, 5 tennis courts, 6 bocce courts, a lakeside boardwalk, a dog park, walking trails, and an on-site bar and grill. A second restaurant has been announced.

What do homes in BeachWalk cost?

The median county just value of a single-family home is about $510,000. The 299 qualified single-family sales in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $715,000, mostly first sales of new homes.
