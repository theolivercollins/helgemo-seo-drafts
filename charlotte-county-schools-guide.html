Charlotte County schools at a glance

An A district, with every school graded C or better.

Charlotte County Public Schools earned an A from the Florida Department of Education for 2025-26, its first district A in 15 years, ranking 21st of Florida's 67 school districts. The state's 2026 counts show 10 A schools, 8 B schools, and 2 C schools in the county, with no D or F schools. The district says its accountability points rose 39 from the prior year.

How Florida grades a school

A percent of possible points, turned into a letter.

Florida grades schools on a percent of possible points earned across measures such as test achievement and learning gains, plus graduation and college and career readiness for high schools. For 2025-26, an elementary school needed 62 percent for an A, a middle school 64 percent, and a high or combination school 65 percent.

That is why this guide shows the percent of points next to each grade. A B at 62 percent and a B at 55 percent are not the same B, and a school one or two points from the next letter is worth noting.

Part 08

Charlotte County school questions, answered

Are Charlotte County schools good?

By the state's measure, yes. Charlotte County Public Schools earned an A from the Florida Department of Education for 2025-26, its first district A in 15 years, and ranked 21st of Florida's 67 districts. Ten schools earned an A, eight a B, and two a C.

Which Charlotte County schools got an A in 2026?

Deep Creek Elementary, Florida SouthWestern Collegiate High School, L.A. Ainger Middle, Lemon Bay High, Liberty Elementary, Meadow Park Elementary, Port Charlotte Middle, Punta Gorda Middle, Sallie Jones Elementary, and Vineland Elementary.

What are the high schools in Port Charlotte?

Port Charlotte High School (grade B for 2025-26) is the district high school located in Port Charlotte. The county has two other district high schools, Charlotte High in Punta Gorda (B) and Lemon Bay High in Englewood (A), and a Port Charlotte address is not automatically zoned for Port Charlotte High, so check the boundary map for the exact address.

What schools serve Punta Gorda?

District schools with Punta Gorda addresses include Sallie Jones Elementary (A), East Elementary (B), Punta Gorda Middle (A), and Charlotte High (B). Deep Creek Elementary (A) serves the Deep Creek area, and Babcock Ranch has the Babcock Neighborhood School charter (B).

Does the ZIP code decide my child's school?

No. Schools are assigned by attendance boundary, which does not follow ZIP codes. Look up the exact address on the district's elementary, middle, and high school boundary pages before you buy.

How does Florida grade schools?

The state converts test results, learning gains, and, for high schools, graduation and college and career readiness into a percent of possible points. Elementary schools need 62 percent for an A, middle schools 64 percent, and high and combination schools 65 percent.
