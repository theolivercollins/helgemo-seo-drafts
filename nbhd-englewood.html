Englewood FL Homes for Sale: Two Counties, One Beach Town, and How to Pick Your Side

Explore · Englewood, Florida

Englewood is an old Gulf Coast fishing town split between two counties, and which side of the line you buy on changes your taxes, your schools, and your commute.

Englewood sits along Lemon Bay, with Manasota Key and the Gulf beaches on the other side of the water. It was platted in 1896 by three brothers from Englewood, Chicago, which is why streets like Dearborn, Harvard, and Yale carry Chicago names. Today it is a census-designated place of about 20,800 people across Sarasota and Charlotte counties. Here is what buyers need to know before they search Englewood FL homes for sale.

The county line

Sarasota side or Charlotte side?

Same town name, different rules.

Englewood is split between Sarasota County to the north and Charlotte County to the south and east. Both counties were created in 1921. The county decides who values your home, what you pay in property taxes, which school district you are in, and which office handles your permits and flood rules.

Homes

Englewood FL homes for sale: what you will find

Mostly 1970s to 1990s homes, plus the beach.

Most Englewood homes are older. The median year built in the Englewood CDP is 1982, and only about 19 percent of homes were built in 2000 or later. Expect concrete block ranch homes, villas, manufactured home communities, golf communities, and waterfront homes on Lemon Bay and the canals that feed it.

Daily life

What living in Englewood is like

Beach, bay, and a two-block downtown.

Daily life in Englewood centers on the water. Manasota Key's beaches sit across Lemon Bay, Stump Pass Beach State Park guards the south end of the key, and the small shopping district along West Dearborn Street is the town's main street. Englewood has its own water utility, the Englewood Water District, and its own fire district.

Stump Pass Beach State Park has a mile of beach where seashells and shark teeth wash up, and it charges $3 per vehicle. Boating is a big part of life here, with Lemon Bay connecting to the Gulf through Stump Pass. Plan your fishing and boating with the tide charts, since parts of the bay are shallow.

Schools

Schools in Englewood

A-rated public schools on both sides, with a cross-county agreement.

Lemon Bay High School and L.A. Ainger Middle School in Charlotte County, and Englewood Elementary School in Sarasota County, all earned A grades from the Florida Department of Education for 2025-26. The two school districts have an interlocal agreement that lets some students attend schools across the county line.

Storms

Hurricanes, flood zones, and insurance

2024 was a hard year here.

In 2024, heavy rain from Hurricane Helene was followed less than two weeks later by storm surge from Hurricane Milton, which caused extensive damage in Englewood. Some homes flooded in Helene and were destroyed in Milton. Ask every seller about storm damage, repairs, and permits, and check the FEMA flood zone before you fall for a house.

FAQs

Englewood: common questions

Is Englewood, Florida in Sarasota or Charlotte County?

Both. Englewood is a census-designated place that spans Sarasota and Charlotte counties. The county sets your property appraiser, tax bill, schools, and permitting, so check it on every listing.

What ZIP codes cover Englewood, FL?

Englewood uses ZIP codes 34223, 34224, and 34295. ZIP 34223 includes the Sarasota County side and Manasota Key, and 34224 covers much of the Charlotte County side east of Lemon Bay.

How much are homes in Englewood, FL?

The Census Bureau's 2020-2024 survey puts the median owner-reported home value at $339,900 for the Englewood CDP, $408,000 for ZIP 34223, and $287,100 for ZIP 34224. Waterfront and Manasota Key homes run well above those medians.

Is Englewood mostly retirees?

Englewood skews older. The median age in the Englewood CDP is 68.5 in the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 survey, and the 2020 census found 55.2 percent of residents were 65 or older.

What schools serve Englewood?

Lemon Bay High School and L.A. Ainger Middle School, both in Charlotte County, earned A grades for 2025-26, as did Englewood Elementary on the Sarasota side. An interlocal agreement lets some students cross the county line, so confirm the assignment for each address.

Did Englewood flood in Hurricanes Helene and Milton?

Yes, parts did. Heavy rain from Hurricane Helene in 2024 was followed less than two weeks later by storm surge from Hurricane Milton, which caused extensive damage in Englewood. Check the flood zone, elevation, and storm history of any home.
