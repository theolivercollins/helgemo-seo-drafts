Explore · Venice · Sarasota National

Sarasota National is a gated golf community of 1,584 homes on 2,400 acres, and more than half of that land is set aside as preserve and open space.

It sits off US 41 southeast of Venice, and the biggest choice for buyers is which of its two villages to buy in. Here is what the community and the county record show. For the wider area, see Explore Venice.

The short version

Big and green. 2,400 acres, with more than 1,300 acres of preserve, conservation, and open space.

Two villages. Golf Village homes include golf membership; Lake Village homes do not.

No age limit. A resort-style community with no age restriction.

CDD on the tax bill. Infrastructure is run by a community development district.

Golf and amenities

The course and the club

An 18-hole course and a big clubhouse campus.

Sarasota National Golf Club's 18-hole championship course is an Audubon International Certified Signature Sanctuary, surrounded by native wetlands and preserves. The fact sheet describes a 32,000-square-foot clubhouse campus with formal and casual dining, fitness, spa treatment rooms, a resort-style pool with 25-meter lap lanes and a beach entry, bocce, pickleball, tennis, a playground, and a separate dog park.

Amenities are for members only. The golf course allows limited public play in the summer months only.

Costs

Fees, CDD, and rentals

Know these before you write an offer.

HOA assessments are billed quarterly and depend on the home type and lot size. Multi-family homes also pay their own sub-association. There is no buyer application or initiation fee, but a one-time $5,000 capital contribution is due at closing. CDD fees appear on the annual tax bill.

FAQs

Sarasota National: common questions

Where is Sarasota National?

Sarasota National is in unincorporated Sarasota County with a Venice address, ZIP 34293. Its main entrance is on US 41, about a mile and a half east of Jacaranda Boulevard, and it is less than seven miles from downtown Venice.

Is golf membership included at Sarasota National?

It depends on the village. Homes in the Golf Village include a golf membership, and homes in the Lake Village do not. All residents can use the community's amenities.

Is Sarasota National a 55+ community?

No. The community says it has no age restrictions.

Does Sarasota National have a CDD?

Yes. The Sarasota National Community Development District builds, operates, and maintains community infrastructure. CDD fees appear on the annual property tax bill and vary by neighborhood.

Can you rent out a home in Sarasota National?

The community allows rentals of 30 days or more, up to four times a year. Rental application and transfer fees may apply.

What do homes in Sarasota National cost?

The county's median just value for a single-family home here is about $449,000. The 112 qualified single-family sales recorded in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $588,000.
