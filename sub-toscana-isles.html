Explore · Venice · Toscana Isles

Toscana Isles is one of the newer neighborhoods inside the City of Venice, with about 968 homes built since 2015 north of Laurel Road.

It is a single community development district of about 417.7 acres, and every home here is less than a dozen years old. Here is what the district and the county record show. For the wider area, see Explore Venice.

Location

Where Toscana Isles sits

Between Knights Trail and the Venetian Golf and River Club.

The district describes its land as east of Knights Trail Road, west of the Venetian Golf and River Club, north of Laurel Road, and south of Gene Green Road, entirely within the City of Venice. Because it is in the city, permits and many local services run through the City of Venice rather than Sarasota County.

Source: Toscana Isles CDD, about the district.

Costs

The CDD and the HOA

Two separate bills to understand.

The Toscana Isles Community Development District is a local special-purpose government created by the Venice City Council in 2013. Its public improvements include clearing and earthwork, storm sewer, sanitary sewer, water mains, irrigation, the entry, walls, buffers, and landscaping. CDD charges show up on the property tax bill, separate from any homeowners association dues.

FAQs

Toscana Isles: common questions

Where is Toscana Isles?

Toscana Isles is inside the City of Venice, east of Knights Trail Road, west of the Venetian Golf and River Club, north of Laurel Road, and south of Gene Green Road. Homes carry a Nokomis 34275 mailing address even though they are in the city.

Does Toscana Isles have a CDD?

Yes. The Toscana Isles Community Development District was established by the Venice City Council on December 10, 2013. It covers about 417.7 acres and finances and maintains infrastructure such as stormwater, water and sewer lines, irrigation, entry features, walls, buffers, and landscaping.

How many homes are in Toscana Isles?

The Sarasota County parcel roll shows about 968 homes in the Toscana Isles plats: 912 single-family homes and 56 carriage homes recorded as condos, built from 2015 to 2024.

Where is the Toscana Isles amenity center?

The CDD holds its meetings at the Toscana Isles Amenity Center, 100 Maraviya Boulevard, Venice.

What do homes in Toscana Isles cost?

The county's median just value for a single-family home here is about $424,000. The 88 qualified single-family sales recorded in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $574,000.
