Choices and notes

• Top 8 drafted now: Boca Grande, Englewood, Wellen Park, Placida and Cape Haze, Manasota Key, Seminole Lakes, Heron Creek, Deep Creek.

• Lake Suzy (rank 5) skipped for now: Lake Suzy is mostly in DeSoto County, has no Census place or Wikipedia article of its own, and its ZIP (34269) is an Arcadia ZIP, so there were too few verifiable facts for a 900+ word page. It stays in the next batch once we can pull county records.

• Twin Isles (rank 9) vs Deep Creek (rank 10): scores were close; Deep Creek was drafted first because its terms carry buyer intent ('deep creek punta gorda homes for sale', 320/mo) while 'twin isles country club' (6,600/mo) is mostly golfers looking for tee times.

• Cannibalization watch: Burnt Store Marina, Punta Gorda Isles, and South Gulf Cove already have live blog posts (South Gulf Cove ranks about position 6). Their /explore/ pages should target the 'homes for sale' phrase and link to the post, not repeat it.

• Venice FL homes for sale (9,900/mo, KD 0) was checked but left off: 8 of the top 10 are portals, and it is outside the Charlotte County core.

• thehelgemoteam.com already ranks #6 for 'boca grande real estate' and #9 for 'punta gorda historic district' in the localized SERP, a good sign those topics are winnable for the brand.

• Charlotte Park (140/mo), Solana (no volume), Harbour Heights (260), Tropical Gulf Acres (370, 7 portals) rank low on demand; keep them as short P3 pages later.
