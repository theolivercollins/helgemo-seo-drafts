Brian Helgemo, Punta Gorda REALTOR®

Founding Agent, REALTOR® · Punta Gorda, FL

Brian Helgemo is a founding agent of The Helgemo Team and a Florida REALTOR® based in downtown Punta Gorda. He has been a licensed Florida real estate sales associate since 2002.

Real estate is in Brian's blood. His grandmother was among the first women Realtors in the state of Florida. Today, his uncle owns and operates the firm that his grandmother started all those years ago. "My dad practiced real estate law early in his career. He also owned and sold many properties through the years, both by himself and with his brothers," he says. "It's definitely a big part of the family legacy."

After earning a degree in psychology and working in sales, Brian received his Florida real estate license on November 25, 2002. The license, SL3041311, is current and active as a sales associate. He is a founding agent of The Helgemo Team, which began that same year, and he still works from the team's office at 320 Cross Street, in historic downtown Punta Gorda.

He works with customers who are buying or selling in Southwest Florida, including people relocating here, looking at waterfront homes, buying a first or second home, or considering an investment property. Local business press, including Gulfshore Business, has quoted him on the Charlotte County housing market.

If you want a feel for how he thinks about this area, start with the guides below. They cover the questions people actually ask before they buy, from canal living in Punta Gorda Isles to condo documents and the layout of Rotonda West.
