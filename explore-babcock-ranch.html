Babcock Ranch Homes for Sale and Area Guide

Punta Gorda Area · Babcock Ranch, FL

Babcock Ranch is a new town built from scratch on old ranch land east of Punta Gorda, and its developer reports it ranked fourth among the nation's top-selling master-planned communities in 2025, according to RCLCO and John Burns.

Its developer calls it America's first solar-powered town. It spans the Charlotte County and Lee County line, and the developer reported more than 15,000 residents and 5,516 homes sold by the end of 2025. The town

What Babcock Ranch is

A planned town, not a subdivision.

Babcock Ranch is an 18,000-acre master-planned town on the Charlotte and Lee county line, planned for 20,000 residences, 60,000 people, and 6 million square feet of commercial space. About 9,000 acres are set aside for green space, parks, and lakes, and it borders the 73,000-acre Babcock Ranch Preserve.

The town center is Founder's Square on Lake Babcock, with shops, restaurants, and community events. Two Florida Power & Light solar energy centers on 870 acres, with about 687,000 panels and 150 megawatts of combined capacity, are why the developer calls it solar powered. The developer also says homes get gigabit fiber internet and that trails are planned for more than 100 miles.

Homes

Babcock Ranch homes for sale: types and price ranges

Almost everything is new construction from national builders.

Babcock Ranch homes include single-family homes, villas, townhomes, coach homes, and rental apartments. The developer says builder prices run from the $200,000s to over $4 million. Resales of homes built since the first residents arrived in 2018 are also part of the market.

Costs

Assessments and fees beyond the mortgage

Two community charges, plus your neighborhood.

Babcock Ranch owners pay a Master Residential Assessment, due quarterly on January 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1, and separate Babcock Ranch Independent Special District assessments that appear on the county property tax bill as non-ad valorem assessments. Some neighborhoods may also have their own association fees.

Schools

Schools in Babcock Ranch

Charter schools inside the town.

Babcock Neighborhood School is a K-8 public charter school that earned a B for 2025-26 from the Florida Department of Education, after an A the year before. The town also has Babcock High, a charter high school, and Bloom Academy for children from 6 weeks to 12 years old.

Check district school assignments for the exact address too. Compare grades across the county in our Charlotte County schools guide.

Storms

How Babcock Ranch handles hurricanes

The developer's claims, and what to verify yourself.

The developer describes Babcock Ranch as built about 30 feet above sea level, with underground utilities and on-site solar and battery storage. It says the town did not lose power or internet and did not flood during Hurricanes Ian in 2022 and Helene and Milton in 2024. Those are the developer's statements, so check the flood zone and insurance quote for any specific home.

Insurance is priced home by home. Our guides to Florida homeowners insurance cost in Punta Gorda and the wind mitigation inspection explain what moves a quote. The Department of Financial Services says homes built to the 2001 Florida Building Code or later qualify for at least a 68 percent discount on the windstorm part of the premium.

FAQs

Babcock Ranch: common questions

Where is Babcock Ranch, Florida?

Babcock Ranch is a master-planned town of about 18,000 acres on the Charlotte County and Lee County line in Southwest Florida, about 15 miles from downtown Fort Myers according to the developer. Charlotte County addresses there use ZIP 33982.

How many people live in Babcock Ranch?

The developer reported more than 15,000 residents and 5,516 homes sold as of December 31, 2025. The town is planned for up to 20,000 residences.

How much are homes in Babcock Ranch?

The developer says homes from national builders range from the $200,000s to over $4 million, including single-family homes, villas, townhomes, and coach homes. Builder pricing changes often, so check current listings and builder price sheets.

Does Babcock Ranch have HOA fees?

Yes, in two layers. Owners pay a Master Residential Assessment, billed quarterly, and the Babcock Ranch Independent Special District charges assessments that appear on the county tax bill as non-ad valorem assessments. Some neighborhoods may add their own association fees, so ask.

What schools are in Babcock Ranch?

Babcock Neighborhood School, a K-8 charter school, earned a B from the state for 2025-26 after an A the year before. The town also has Babcock High, a charter high school, and Bloom Academy for early childhood.

Did Babcock Ranch flood in Hurricane Ian?

The developer says that through Hurricanes Ian, Helene, and Milton the town did not lose power or internet and did not flood. It describes the town as built about 30 feet above sea level with underground utilities. Check the FEMA flood zone for any specific home.
