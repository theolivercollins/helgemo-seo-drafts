Punta Gorda & Charlotte County, FL

A Punta Gorda mailing address can mean a downtown bungalow, a canal home in Punta Gorda Isles, a marina village on the Lee County line, or a new town east of the interstate.

That is four very different lives under one city name, and the ZIP code is the fastest way to tell them apart. It is also one of the most misunderstood details on a listing, because the mailing city and the city limits are not the same thing.

Here is how the Punta Gorda ZIP codes actually break down, with the public data behind each one.

The short version

Four residential ZIPs. Punta Gorda uses 33950, 33955, 33982, and 33983. A fifth, 33951, is used for post office boxes.

33950 is the core. Downtown, the Historic District, Punta Gorda Isles, and Burnt Store Isles all use it.

A Punta Gorda address is not a city address. Only 33950 and 33955 overlap the city limits in Census Bureau data. 33982 and 33983 are unincorporated county.

The spread is real. Median owner-reported values run from $314,700 in 33982 to $433,800 in 33950, ACS 2020-2024.

Part 02

Mailing city vs city limits

Why the word Punta Gorda on a listing can mislead you.

A Punta Gorda mailing address does not mean a home is inside the city. In the Census Bureau's 2020 relationship file, only ZIP areas 33950 and 33955 overlap the city of Punta Gorda, and both also include unincorporated land. Every 33982 and 33983 address is in unincorporated Charlotte County, which changes who provides services and which taxes appear on the bill.

The U.S. Postal Service draws ZIP codes to deliver mail, not to mark city limits. Punta Gorda is the only incorporated city in Charlotte County, with 20,278 residents in the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 estimates. Add up the four residential Punta Gorda ZIP areas and you get roughly 68,800 people, more than three times the city's population. Most of the difference lives in unincorporated Charlotte County.

Why it matters to a buyer: a home inside the city limits is served and taxed by the City of Punta Gorda as well as the county. In the city's canal neighborhoods, such as Punta Gorda Isles and Burnt Store Isles, the city maintains the seawalls through canal assessment districts, and that assessment shows up on the tax bill. A home a few miles away with the same mailing city may have none of that. The parcel record on the Charlotte County Property Appraiser site shows the taxing district for any address, and I check it before a customer writes an offer.

Read the address carefully

Four ZIP codes share the Punta Gorda name. Only two of them reach inside the city limits.

The reverse happens too. Some public records list 33983 addresses with Port Charlotte as the city. The National Center for Education Statistics, for example, lists Deep Creek Elementary at a Port Charlotte 33983 address, while the Deep Creek Civic Association uses Punta Gorda 33983. When you research a Deep Creek home, search county records under both names.

ZIP by ZIP: what each one feels like on the ground

Four profiles, from the harbor to the ranch lands.

33950 is the walkable, water-focused city core with the oldest median age. 33955 is the area south of the city along US 41 and Burnt Store Road, home to marina and golf communities. 33982 is by far the largest by land, about 407 square miles, and includes Babcock Ranch. 33983 is Deep Creek, a year-round residential community of lakes and single-family streets north of the Peace River.

How to use ZIP codes in a home search

A ZIP narrows the search. It does not finish it.

Use the ZIP code as a first filter, then confirm five things for the exact address: whether it is inside the city limits, the FEMA flood zone, the assigned schools, any association or community fees, and the homestead situation if it will be your primary home. Each of those is set parcel by parcel, not by ZIP.

01

City or county

Check the taxing district on the Property Appraiser's parcel record. City parcels can carry city services and assessments, such as canal maintenance in the canal neighborhoods.

Part 06

Punta Gorda ZIP code questions, answered

What is the zip code for Punta Gorda, Florida?

Punta Gorda uses four residential ZIP codes: 33950, 33955, 33982, and 33983. 33950 is the city core, including downtown and Punta Gorda Isles. 33951 is used for post office boxes.

What zip code is downtown Punta Gorda?

33950. Downtown, the Historic District, Punta Gorda Isles, and Burnt Store Isles all use 33950. Our office at 320 Cross Street is in 33950 too.

What zip code is Babcock Ranch?

Babcock Ranch addresses use 33982, the large east-county ZIP that also covers the Punta Gorda Airport area. Addresses there may be written as Babcock Ranch or Punta Gorda.

What zip code is Deep Creek?

Deep Creek uses 33983. Some public records list 33983 addresses as Port Charlotte rather than Punta Gorda, so search both city names when you look up records.

Does a Punta Gorda address mean the home is inside the city limits?

No. In the Census Bureau's 2020 relationship file, only ZIP areas 33950 and 33955 overlap the city of Punta Gorda. Many 33950 and 33955 addresses, and all 33982 and 33983 addresses, are in unincorporated Charlotte County. Check the parcel on the Property Appraiser's site.

Which Punta Gorda zip code has the highest home values?

33950. Its ACS 2020-2024 median owner-reported home value is $433,800, against $376,200 for 33955, $363,400 for 33983, and $314,700 for 33982. Those are survey estimates, not sale prices.

Does the ZIP code decide which school my child attends?

No. Charlotte County Public Schools assigns schools by address and attendance boundary, not by ZIP. Check the exact address with the district's boundary maps before you buy.
