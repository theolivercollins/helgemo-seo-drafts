Explore · Venice · Pelican Pointe

Pelican Pointe is a gated golf community of 1,354 home sites on about 1,000 acres, with three nines of golf winding through preserves and lakes.

Its homes went up from the mid-1990s through 2018, according to the county record. Here is what the club and the county record show, and what buyers should check. For the wider area, see Explore Venice.

The club

Golf and amenities

Three nines, a big pool, and new pickleball courts.

The club describes 27 holes of semi-private championship golf through nature preserves and lakes. The practice facility was re-leveled and regrassed in 2025, with 25 turf hitting stations, an 8,000-square-foot putting green, and separate chipping and bunker areas. There is a heated Junior Olympic pool that is covered in winter, two tennis courts, four dedicated pickleball courts that opened in 2026, and a fitness room.

The 22,000-square-foot clubhouse handles dining, golf banquets, weddings, and club activities. The club says bald eagles are among the wildlife on the property.

FAQs

Pelican Pointe: common questions

Where is Pelican Pointe?

Pelican Pointe Golf and Country Club is in unincorporated Sarasota County with a Venice address. Most of its parcels use ZIP 34285 and the rest use 34292.

How big is Pelican Pointe?

The club describes a 1,000-acre property with 1,354 home sites within a gated entrance, plus natural preserves, wetlands, and lakes.

Is the golf course private?

The club describes its 27 holes as semi-private. Ask the club how golf and social memberships work for homeowners.

What amenities does Pelican Pointe have?

The club lists a 22,000-square-foot clubhouse, a heated Junior Olympic pool, two tennis courts, four dedicated pickleball courts opened in 2026, a fitness room, and a practice facility regrassed in 2025.

What do homes in Pelican Pointe cost?

The county's median just value for a single-family home here is about $468,000, and about $265,000 for an attached villa. The 75 qualified single-family sales recorded in 2025 and 2026 had a median of about $630,000.
